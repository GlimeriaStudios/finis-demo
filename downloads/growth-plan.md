# Swedish acquisition and launch plan

Research date: 13 October 2026. **Active market: Sweden; language: Swedish; currency: SEK; timezone: Europe/Stockholm.** The superseded UK plan is preserved only in `archive/superseded-uk-2026-10-13/`. This owner-facing analysis is English; campaign keywords and ads are Swedish. No accounts, imports or spending were created.

## Positioning

Build an original Swedish alternative to Wint for owner-managed service aktiebolag with straightforward accounting needs. Wint markets automated bookkeeping for AB and states its proposition includes bokslut, årsredovisning, momsdeklarationer and arbetsgivardeklarationer. Competing requires actual local integrations and competent service delivery, not just a dashboard. [Wint](https://www.wint.se/), [How Wint works](https://www.wint.se/sa-funkar-det).

Compare identical specimen businesses: AB, annual turnover, transaction count, employees, VAT period, accounting method, banks/currencies, quality of records and year-end. Obtain dated comparable quotes. Compare software, labour, payroll, bokslut, årsredovisning, INK2, personal K10, onboarding, exclusions, support and term separately. Software-only products are not equivalent to managed accounting. Do not claim cheapest, unlimited service, professional authorisation or savings without evidence. Do not reproduce Wint's identity, protected content or private software.

Initial differentiation hypothesis: clear scope, Swedish support, understandable onboarding, explicit task ownership and usable exports. Test with eligible real customers. More customers than competitors is an ambition, not a promise inferable from ad impressions.

## Price and offer

The original economic reference remains **£200/month, first three months free; £600 once for year-end work**. These are GBP references, not Swedish checkout prices. **Final monthly and year-end SEK prices are TBD**. No GBP/SEK observation was verified. Never silently turn them into 200 kr or 600 kr or present an invented conversion as confirmed.

Save a dated Riksbank GBP/SEK reference observation, verify quote direction and calculate a correctly oriented conversion before proposing SEK prices. Select commercially rounded prices only after local delivery economics and moms treatment are reviewed. Riksbank rates are indicative, not transaction quotes. [Riksbank rates](https://www.riksbank.se/sv/statistik/rantor-och-valutakurser/sok-rantor-och-valutakurser/).

For chosen monthly SEK price P net of moms, monthly delivery cost C, onboarding O and acquisition A, first-year contribution for a 12-month retained customer is **9P − 12C − O − A**, before fixed overhead. Original GBP arithmetic yields £1,800 first-year receipts rather than £2,400; that is not an FX conversion. For one-off work, subtract actual delivery/review/support and acquisition cost from the chosen net SEK price; do not apply recurring lifetime value.

Define the trial's software versus human-service inclusions, entity eligibility, transaction limit, payroll, VAT, banks/currencies, cancellation, catch-up work and deadlines. Bokslut, årsredovisning, inkomstdeklaration and K10 are different deliverables. A free period creates real delivery cost before cash receipts. Do not advertise included filings or automatic integrations before they work.

## Analytics, accounts and Swedish consent

Create organisation-owned GA4 and Ads accounts when owner access, true legal identity, billing details and domain exist. Use SEK, Europe/Stockholm, Sweden targeting and Swedish ads. Advertiser verification/payment profile must match real legal details; targeting Sweden does not authorise inventing a Swedish company/address. Keep IDs blank until real accounts exist. Linking code is not account creation. [GA4 setup](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/14183469?hl=en).

Use basic consent mode: no GA/Ads script/network request before opt-in; analytics_storage, ad_storage, ad_user_data and ad_personalization default denied. Swedish controls: Acceptera alla, Avvisa valfria, Inställningar, with separate Statistik and Marknadsföring and easy withdrawal. PTS requires active, revocable consent and information for non-necessary cookies; analytics is not automatically necessary. **Do not import the superseded UK statistical exception.** [PTS cookie rules](https://www.pts.se/internet-och-telefoni/kakor-cookies/).

IMY explains voluntary, specific GDPR consent and purpose separation. Distinguish optional marketing consent from lawful bases needed to provide accounting services. Review processor agreements, international transfers, retention and rights using actual data flows. Never send personnummer, payroll, bank details, contact details, receipts or tax records to analytics. [IMY consent](https://www.imy.se/verksamhet/dataskydd/det-har-galler-enligt-gdpr/rattslig-grund/samtycke/).

Consent mode controls tags; it is not the consent banner. Keep marketing measurement outside authenticated financial screens. See `marketing/measurement-plan.md`. [Google consent setup](https://developers.google.com/tag-platform/security/guides/consent).

## Swedish operational work before selling

1. Establish true legal/trading identity, organisationsnummer where applicable, contact details, relevant F-skatt/moms registration, terms, cancellation, complaints and professional responsibility. Clarify AB versus enskild firma. Do not claim auktoriserad status or professional membership without evidence.
2. Implement verifications, chronological/systematic records, corrections, reconciliation, audit history and archival access. Accounting records generally need preservation through the seventh year after the calendar year in which the financial year ended, subject to detailed rules. Cancellation cannot blindly delete required records. [Skatteverket bookkeeping](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretagorganisationer/startadrivaavslutaforetag/bokforingbokslut/bokforingvadkraverlagen.4.18e1b10334ebe8bc80005195.html), [Retention](https://www4.skatteverket.se/rattsligvagledning/edition/2026.9/324690.html).
3. Apply appropriate BFN framework and period rules. Do not assume every small company can use K2, or a dashboard export is an årsredovisning. Review current K2/K3 rules including 2026 changes with a Swedish practitioner. [Bokföringsnämnden](https://www.bfn.se/).
4. Confirm AML scope and registration with Bolagsverket as applicable, plus supervision, risk assessment, customer/beneficial-owner checks, routines and reporting. [Bolagsverket AML register](https://bolagsverket.se/omoss/flerverksamheter/registretmotpenningtvatt.2557.html), [Länsstyrelsen guidance](https://www.lansstyrelsen.se/vastmanland/samhalle/betalning-ekonomi-och-pengar/forhindra-penningtvatt-och-finansiering-av-terrorism.html).
5. Customers must grant appropriate Skatteverket ombud permissions; customers retain responsibility for correctness. Verify authority and signing workflows. Never label a simulated action a filed declaration. [Skatteverket ombud](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/drivaforetag/ombudforettforetag/utseombudforettforetag.4.5a85666214dbad743ff1158c.html).
6. Bank feeds/payment initiation require appropriate regulated-provider arrangements and assessment of the firm's own role. FI describes permissions and the account-information registration route. An API key is not permission. [Finansinspektionen](https://www.fi.se/sv/betalningar/sok-tillstand/betaltjanster/).
7. Verify authentication, tenant isolation, roles, encryption, audit trail, uploads, backup/restore, export, incident handling and financial calculations. Establish Swedish support, reviewer capacity, onboarding ceilings and close deadlines. Do not collect real documents in an unreviewed prototype. Review Swedish accounting archival rules and conditions for storage outside Sweden as well as GDPR hosting/transfer requirements.

## First 90 days after release gates

Days 1–14: confirm SEK price/scope, operational requirements and production privacy/security; onboard a small supervised Swedish pilot; publish accurate service/pricing pages and two useful guides.

Days 15–30: run only the approved capped Swedish Search test; review terms, geography and lead quality; record objections. Test clear inclusions versus easier switching one variable at a time. Monitor qualified lead cost while trial cohorts mature.

Days 31–60: improve migration, support and month-end delivery; create permissioned cases; expand only a service-fit cluster within capacity. Record real labour, support and CAC per cohort.

Days 61–90+: evaluate retention and eventual paid conversion at the correct cohort age. Three free months delay paid-conversion evidence. Scale retained contribution, not signup volume or absolute-top impressions. Offer changes remain explicit reviewed business decisions.
