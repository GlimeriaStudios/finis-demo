# Swedish bookkeeping launch: market analysis and product requirements

Research checked 13 October 2026. This document supersedes the UK-market analysis following the user's explicit instruction to target Sweden. The UK version is preserved in `archive/superseded-uk-research/market-research-uk-2026-10-13.md`; it is not Swedish launch guidance. All suggested offer scope, SEK prices and engineering requirements are proposals, not established business facts or delivered services.

## Executive decision

Target Swedish owner-managed aktiebolag (AB), initially invoice-based consultants, agencies and other straightforward service businesses. Swedish customer-facing copy, SEK presentation, Swedish accounting terminology and actual Swedish delivery capability are essential. A translation of a UK site is insufficient: HMRC, Companies House, PAYE, CT600 and MTD must be removed from the active offer and replaced with the applicable Swedish workflows.

The user's economic offer remains £200/month with the first three months free and £600 for a one-time year-end service. The market change does not authorise changing pounds to 200 kronor. At the checked exchange-rate reference this is approximately **2,645 SEK/month** and **7,935 SEK one-time**. These should be presented as proposed SEK equivalents pending a chosen fixed-SEK contract or explicit GBP billing policy. VAT inclusion was not specified.

This is not the cheapest Swedish software price, and the normal monthly equivalent exceeds Wint's advertised solo-business price. The defensible positioning is clear scope, reliable service, easy migration and a three-month introduction—not an unsupported claim that prices undercut everyone.

## Pricing conversion and commercial basis

The ECB's 13 October 2026 reference table gives EUR 1 = GBP 0.84460 and SEK 11.1698. Our cross-rate calculation is 11.1698 / 0.84460 = **13.22495856 SEK per GBP**. Therefore £200 ≈ SEK2,644.99; £600 ≈ SEK7,934.98; nine paid months in the first year = £1,800 ≈ SEK23,804.93. These are reference calculations, not transaction quotes; the ECB explicitly identifies its rates as informational. [ECB reference rates](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/policy_and_exchange_rates/euro_reference_exchange_rates/html/index.en.html)

Recommended review draft: retain exact £200/£600 commercial source values in configuration, show proposed SEK2,645/SEK7,935 to the owner, and keep checkout disabled until currency/VAT/contract terms are confirmed. A round SEK2,649 price is a separate commercial decision, not an exact conversion. If prices are exclusive of ordinary 25% Swedish VAT, SEK2,645 becomes SEK3,306.25 gross; inclusive pricing would produce a different net service budget. Skatteverket states 25% applies to almost all goods/services, with exceptions. Confirm the actual supplier and transaction treatment rather than blindly applying a universal rate. [Skatteverket VAT rates](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/saljavarorochtjanster/momssatserochundantagfranmoms.4.58d555751259e4d66168000409.html)

## Wint: public feature scope and strategic lessons

Wint describes an action dashboard, invoicing, bank matching, receipts, expense reimbursement, approval-controlled supplier payments, payroll, timesheets, reports, year-end work and expert support. It combines software and operational service; a dashboard recreation does not recreate that delivery organisation. [Wint product](https://www.wint.se/produkten)

Its public support model uses a specialist team rather than one assigned contact for every question. Its migration FAQ describes SIE import and separately quoted catch-up bookkeeping. These indicate that standardisation and clean intake are central to scalability. [Support model](https://www.wint.se/faq-fraga/varfor-far-jag-ingen-kontaktperson-hos-wint), [Migration](https://www.wint.se/faq-fraga/hur-flyttar-jag-over-min-gamla-bokforing-till-wint)

Wint's formation route has Swedish-company eligibility and a 12-month service commitment; do not transfer its “company included” claim to a new business without a supplier and funded delivery. Its partner channel and estate-agent service are distinct growth avenues, requiring more than generic bookkeeping. [Formation](https://www.wint.se/for-vem/starta-bolag), [Partners](https://www.wint.se/redovisningsbyraer), [Estate agents](https://www.wint.se/for-vem/fastighetsmaklare)

Original differentiation proposal: visible monthly close status, accountable support, transparent workload limits, clean SIE migration and easy evidence export. Win an initial consultant/agency segment through reliability and onboarding, then expand. Avoid unsupported customer-count, “best,” certified, authorised or guaranteed tax-saving claims.

## Swedish AB product requirements

These are original implementation requirements informed by official rules, not claims that the prototype already meets them.

| Workflow | Swedish requirement/specification | Production evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Company setup | Organisationsnummer, legal name, financial year, registered tax/employer status, representative/ombud authority | Verified entity and authority records |
| Books | Double-entry journals, verifikationer, account mapping, period locks, corrections and source documents | Reconciled balances and accountant-reviewed fixtures |
| Migration | SIE import/export, opening balances and historical documents | Trial import reconciled to source |
| Invoices | Swedish invoice data, applicable VAT, sequential identity, credits, partial payments | Reviewed templates and tax treatment |
| Bank/skattekonto | Distinct bank events and tax-account transactions; no duplicate booking | Genuine consented integration and reconciliation |
| VAT | Registered period and accounting method; review, declaration and payment tracked separately | Skatteverket receipt and correct period |
| Payroll | Payslips, withholding, employer contributions, benefits, absence and AGI | Actual payroll provider and submission receipts |
| Year end | Eligible K2/K3, bokslut, årsredovisning and company tax work separately | Reviewed documents, signatures and receipts |
| Support | Swedish-language service ownership, escalation and export | Real staff and measured response capacity |

Invoices should be checked against Skatteverket's invoice requirements, including applicable identities, dates, goods/services and tax information. Do not simply replace a UK VAT field label. [Invoice rules](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretagochorganisationer/moms/saljavarorochtjanster/fakturering.4.58d555751259e4d66168000403.html)

### Year end and accounting frameworks

An AB's annual report must reach Bolagsverket within one month after the general meeting approves the accounts, and no later than seven months after financial-year end to avoid late fees. Company tax declaration is a separate Skatteverket workflow; ABs declare after every financial year, including inactive years, and deadlines depend on year end. [Bolagsverket annual reports](https://bolagsverket.se/foretag/aktiebolag/arsredovisningforaktiebolag.759.html), [Skatteverket AB declarations](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/inkomstdeklaration/deklareraatettaktiebolagellerenekonomiskforening.4.46ae6b26141980f1e2d1261.html)

Skatteverket's current page says its older Inkomstdeklaration 2 e-service is being retired 29 December 2026, with other digital routes available. The product must use a supported route; merely generating a file is not proof of accepted filing. [Current service notice](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/inkomstdeklaration/deklareraatettaktiebolagellerenekonomiskforening.4.46ae6b26141980f1e2d1261.html)

K3 is the main framework; eligible smaller ABs may choose K2. Eligibility is not equivalent to “all small companies.” BFN publishes distinct versions for financial years beginning after 31 December 2025 and specific transition conditions. Version the framework by financial year and obtain qualified review. [BFN framework overview](https://www.bfn.se/om-bokforingsnamnden/k-projektet/), [Current K rules](https://www.bfn.se/redovisningsregler/vagledningar/k-regelverk/)

### VAT and payroll

VAT timing depends on registered monthly, quarterly or annual reporting. Declaration and funds credited to the tax account must be tracked correctly; the software should read the company's actual dates rather than assume everyone files quarterly. [Skatteverket VAT deadlines](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/moms/deklareramoms/narskajagdeklareramoms.4.6d02084411db6e252fe80008988.html)

Payroll must support employer declarations, including zero declarations while employer registration remains active, subject to relevant exceptions. Access requires the correct authority and acceptance is demonstrated by a receipt, not an upload button. Rules, benefits and corrections need actual payroll expertise. [Employer-declaration guidance](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/arbetsgivare/lamnaarbetsgivardeklaration.4.41f1c61d16193087d7fcaeb.html)

### Records and permissions

BFN says accounting information is retained for seven years after the calendar year in which the financial year ends. Paper may be destroyed after a transfer only when the transfer safeguards the information against change/loss. Do not repeat outdated generic advice that every photographed paper receipt must remain for four years. [BFN archiving](https://www.bfn.se/fragor-och-svar/arkivering/)

Professional bookkeeping providers can fall within Bolagsverket's anti-money-laundering register requirements. Determine the business's actual obligations, customer checks and supervision before operational launch. Bank information/payment initiation are regulated functions; use an appropriately authorised provider and verify the proposed operating model. [AML register](https://bolagsverket.se/omoss/flerverksamheter/registretmotpenningtvatt.2557.html), [Finansinspektionen payment services](https://www.fi.se/sv/betalningar/sok-tillstand/betaltjanster/)

## Offer economics and scope

Assume the £200 source price is net of tax purely for this calculation. Three free months mean 9 paid months: £1,800 first year versus £2,400 recurring full years. At the reference rate that is approximately SEK23,805 first year and SEK31,740 thereafter. A free quarter is a 25% first-year revenue discount before service costs.

Illustrative assumptions, not competitor benchmarks: monthly delivery cost SEK900, onboarding SEK1,300, acquisition SEK4,000. First-year contribution before fixed overhead is 23,805 − 10,800 − 1,300 − 4,000 = SEK7,705. At delivery cost SEK1,500/month, the same result is SEK505. At SEK1,800/month it is negative SEK3,095. Thus manual workload, trial conversion and retention matter more than signup volume. Track staff minutes, documents, exceptions, support and corrections per customer.

The proposed standard subscription should define one AB, transaction allowance, payroll headcount, included VAT periods, year-end eligibility, advisory limits, existing-record readiness and cancellation/export. Show the first charge date after the free quarter. No hidden retrospective fees. The price cannot safely cover every entity, group, inventory operation or client-money workflow without scoping.

The £600 equivalent year-end offer should explicitly state whether it includes bokslut, årsredovisning and Inkomstdeklaration 2. Proposed eligibility: clean reconciled books, one straightforward AB/year, eligible K2, no audit/group/complex tax work. K10 for an owner is a separate personal-tax matter and should not be silently promised. Existing arrears and corrections should be identified before accepting the job. These are proposed terms requiring review, not user-approved exclusions.

## Swedish acquisition priorities

Use Swedish intent clusters: “bokföring aktiebolag”, “redovisningsbyrå online”, “löpande bokföring fast pris”, “bokslut aktiebolag”, “årsredovisning hjälp”, “byta redovisningsbyrå” and consultant/agency variants. These are hypotheses, not verified search volumes. Validate Swedish geography, language, match types and bids with live Keyword Planner before spending.

Create distinct landing pages for ongoing service, year-end help and switching. Explain the process and price scope; use genuine credentials and real examples only. Build educational content around document readiness, SIE migration and the difference between bokslut, årsredovisning and tax declaration. Avoid mass-produced city pages where there is no local service evidence.

Use actual qualified-lead, onboarding, paid-after-trial and retained-customer events. Acquisition budget must follow observed contribution and service capacity; no public source establishes the spend needed to beat competitors. A initial target of 25 qualified AB customers with measured delivery economics is a stronger launch milestone than claiming national market leadership.

## Outstanding launch evidence

Before activation: actual legal entity and trading name, Swedish-language terms/privacy, GDPR processing/subprocessors, secure tenant storage, credentials and staffing, professional cover, AML obligations, bank/filing partners, VAT/currency decision, approved advertising billing cap, and verified conversion tracking. No account should be created using invented identity or company details.

Research limitations: public pages may change and offers differ by eligibility. The review did not access private Wint software, internal costs, customer databases or conversion data. Quoted prices are snapshots, not binding quotes. The old UK workflow document remains superseded wherever it refers to UK-specific implementation; Swedish requirements here govern the launch.
