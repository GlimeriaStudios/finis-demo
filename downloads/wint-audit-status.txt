Finis — Wint reference audit and implementation status
16 October 2026

Reference inspected: https://wint.saltfish.ai/demobolaget.ab/
The demo advertises limited functionality. No production access was obtained.

Evidence: local screenshot archive with rendered UI snapshots and click-log.json.
All 25 report-directory entries were visited. Forms and alternate states were
inspected for invoices, customers, quotes, projects, time, leave, compensation,
benefits, articles, groups, automations, documents and settings. Task detail
screens include supplier bills, receipts, missing documents, payroll and closing.

Implemented in this revision:
- Searchable 25-entry report directory with six category filters.
- Grouped navigation; Banktransaktioner remains the final sidebar option.
- Dashboard task accordions grouped by type or priority, four quick actions,
  support links, period filters, sales comparison and cost distribution graphs.
- Monthly/accumulated sales with exact-value table and posted-ledger data.
- Invoice editor with saved customer selection, multiple rows, date/VAT section
  and live preview. Draft save uses integer amounts and atomic validation.
- Supplier summary derived from local bills, group membership summary,
  document lists, local register drafts and register CSV exports.

Existing local accounting, receipt review, OCR suggestions, bank CSV matching,
SIE import/export, quotes, recurring drafts, time/project billing and manual
payroll preview remain available. These are demo workflows, not live services.

Not complete / not production parity:
- K10 calculations, filing, statutory annual accounts and tax automation.
- Live bank access, payment initiation, cards, staffed advice and payroll payout.
- Real invoice email/e-invoice delivery, document sharing and access roles.
- Automatic execution of receipt rules; new automation records are drafts only.
- Article reuse, mixed VAT by invoice row, foreign currency, all unit/category
  options, periodisation, supplier auto-approval and all reminder settings.
- Full asset/depreciation processing, paid contract templates and signing.
- Every filter/sort permutation, every record, role and validation path.

Validation: 47 automated tests pass. Browser checks verified all 25 report
destinations render, report search and balance deep-link, task priority toggle,
chart accumulation toggle, mobile navigation and a two-line invoice draft
totalling 237.53 SEK. No real invoice, payment or support message was sent.

This is an audited partial reconstruction, not an exact complete replica.
